Controls

Stick (bottom-left) — pitch / roll. Drag up to climb, down to descend; drag left / right to bank. Release to centre.
Throttle (left slider) — W / S, or Shift / Ctrl.
Flaps / Airbrake / Vector — right panel sliders. Vector only on the F-35A: at 90° the throttle becomes lift — hover, then descend under 4 m/s onto a VTOL pad.
Keyboard — Stick arrow keys (pitch / roll) · Rudder Q / E · Throttle W / S (or Shift / Ctrl) · Flaps [ / ] (±10°) · Airbrake X (toggle) · Gear G · Hook H · Brake hold B · Flare F · Fire gun hold Space · Missile M (needs lock — hold the target in the 15° cone, listen for the tone) · Bomb N (F-18 / F-15E only) · Camera C · Camera reset R · Pause P. Mouse: drag on the canvas steers (mouse yoke) in Chase cam. Tilt your phone (📱 Tilt button — iOS asks permission first, tap again to re-centre). Gamepad: left stick, triggers throttle, A fire, B missile, X flare, Y gear, Start pause.
Stick feel: the keyboard stick ramps like a real stick, not a switch — pitch slews at 5/s, roll snaps over at 10/s and returns at 3/s, so a tap banks the jet. The touch stick's authority falls with speed, so a full drag stays gentle at high speed. Pitch / roll inversion and the 0.5×–2.0× sensitivity exponents are set in the setup menu (🎮 Controls) and apply to the keyboard arrows.
Camera C — Chase / Free orbit (drag to orbit, pinch to zoom in Free). R resets the camera.
Pause P · Restart ↻ button · Setup (pause menu) — change the sandbox settings mid-flight and Apply & Restart, or Resume with the old settings.
Spawn: Alpha, Bravo or Charlie runway, or the Helicarrier deck (setup menu) — the jet starts on the deck at the carrier's heading and speed, ready to roll off the bow.
Carrier trap: hook down, under 140 kt over one of the three stern wires — the wire catches you and hauls you to a stop. Over that speed it snaps.
VTOL pad: F-35A only — vector 70°+, slow vertical descent onto a marked yellow pad on the deck.
Lead pipper: the gold ring on the HUD is where your gun rounds will meet the locked target — put the ring on the jet, not the jet itself.
ROTATE: the green cue appears on the runway roll at your jet's rotation speed — hold ↑ (keyboard) or drag the stick up and HOLD it, not before, and not a tap.
Pull up: diving with bombs below the minimum-release altitude warns PULL UP — the 4 g pull-out needs that height.
Spin: if the jet departs into a spin the HUD shows an arrow — push the stick WITH the arrow, nose down, and the spin unwinds.
Stall: below stall speed the wing drops and the nose falls — push the nose down and add power to recover.
Damage: wing hits roll you, engine hits cost thrust. Smoke under 60%, fire under 35%.
Minimap: green = runways, blue = helicarrier, red diamonds = enemies, grey = traffic, white = you.
