## First, the honest caveat — Hurricane Fleet jets are NOT player-flown

The player steers ships. Jets are AI squadrons commanded from the Air Ops panel (launch / recall only; `CarrierAirWingSystem.launch`, line ~6560). There is **no stick input, no throttle, no dead zone, no sensitivity curve for jets** in Hurricane Fleet. What transfers to Pandora Planes is therefore the *feel model* — how a jet banks, turns, bleeds speed, lands and pulls out — not an input mapping. Where the BET report (`Docs/flight-behaviour-research.md`) gives a real-world number, it wins; where Hurricane Fleet gives a proven feel target, it is marked ADOPT/ADAPT.

Also note Hurricane Fleet's unit scale is loose: its effective gravity is ~10–15 u/s² in different places (pull-out 47 u/s² labelled "4 g" → g≈11.75; crash dive 25 u/s²; bank formula uses g=15). 1 unit ≈ 1.1–1.5 m. **Never port raw HF constants — port the formulas, re-anchored to metres and g = 9.81.**

## Numbers the builder should use (Hornet, and the other jets by spec)

- **Coordinated bank:** φ = atan(V·ω/g), g = 9.81; roll-rate limit 220°/s; AI bank clamp ±75°.
- **Sustained turn:** emerges from k = 1/(π·e·AR) per spec (F-18 k ≈ 0.10) + thrust lapse (ρ/ρ₀)^0.7 → ~19°/s SL, ~12.7°/s at 15,000 ft for the F-18. HF's lateral-cap mechanism independently lands at 18.7°/s at cruise — cross-check passed.
- **Speed bleed:** from BET induced + separation drag, not a hardcoded subtraction; feel target is HF's (n−1) shape.
- **Pitch:** q-scheduled, max 55°/s, stick slew 3/s; rotation 10–15°/s at Vr (F-18 126 kt, F-22 140, F-15E 160, F-35A 150).
- **Carrier pattern:** downwind → base → final; glide slope 3.5–4°; trap ~140 kt; wire catch + rollout decel; gear down from base leg onward; 8° AoA on final.
- **Catapult:** end speed ~140 kt, ~2 s stroke, pitch 10–12° off the bow; deck-motion safety gate.
- **Bombing:** 30° dive, 4 g level pull-out, wings level in pull-out; release cue from the Δh formula (CCIP pipper).
- **AI response lags (from HF, user-approved feel):** heading 3/s, turn-rate smoothing 6/s.
- **Crash:** dying spin per the War Thunder report (yaw ≤ ~90°/s, descent 50–120 m/s), fire/smoke trail, impact explosion scaled by remaining fuel.
